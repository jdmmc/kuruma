The weight is at the back. You drive it with your right foot.重さは後ろに。操るのは右足で。

The engine sits behind the rear axle, so 60% of the weight is on the rear tyres. Get on the throttle and it digs in and launches hard; lift and the tail starts to come round. A stiffer front anti-roll bar keeps it calmer at the limit.エンジンを後軸より後ろに積んだ RR。荷重の 60% が後輪に掛かるので、踏めば強く蹴り出し、抜けば尻が回り込みます。前のスタビを厚くして、限界での巻き込みは和らげてあります。

Engineエンジン

420 N·m / 4,400 rpm

A high-revving engine in the spirit of a flat six: peak torque at 4,400 rpm, peak power at 6,500, and it pulls to 7,400. Low and slippery, it has the highest top speed of the three.水平対向 6 気筒をイメージした、高回転まで回るエンジン。4,400rpm で最大トルク、6,500rpm で最高出力、7,400rpm まで回ります。背が低く空気抵抗が小さいので、最高速は 3 台でいちばん伸びます。

With the weight at the back, the rear tyres get loaded under acceleration and it puts power down well. Lift mid-corner, though, and the tail wants to step out.
